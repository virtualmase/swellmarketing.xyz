Core capabilities

Four disciplines. One outcome.

The goal is not more activity. It is a clearer, better-supported brand representation wherever an AI system gathers evidence.

01 · Diagnose

GEO audit & baseline

Establish what AI systems can access, how they describe your brand, and which competitors currently own relevant answers.

  • Entity and schema review
  • Crawler and indexability checks
  • Prompt-set citation baseline
  • Prioritized remediation plan
02 · Clarify

Entity architecture

Create one stable, corroborated description of your organization, people, offers, expertise, and relationships.

  • JSON-LD entity graph
  • Canonical identifiers
  • sameAs verification map
  • robots.txt, sitemap, and llms.txt
03 · Prove

Citable content system

Turn expertise into pages with clear claims, useful definitions, supporting evidence, and strong internal relationships.

  • Topic and intent architecture
  • Pillar and supporting pages
  • Answer-ready passages
  • Editorial evidence standards
04 · Improve

Authority & measurement

Strengthen independent corroboration, then track the questions and platforms that matter to the business.

  • Authority gap analysis
  • Earned distribution roadmap
  • Answer accuracy tracking
  • Share of Model reporting
