Five dimensions

Audit the whole system.

A brand can have excellent content and still be hard to identify, crawl, corroborate, or measure. Work through the dimensions in order; upstream ambiguity can distort everything after it.

01

Entity clarity

Names, identifiers, structured data, people, offers, and relationships agree.

02

Crawler access

Important pages, facts, policies, and sitemaps are available to relevant systems.

03

Content evidence

Key claims are specific, supported, extractable, and connected to useful answers.

04

Independent authority

External sources corroborate the expertise and facts you want associated with the brand.

05

Answer measurement

A stable query set tracks accuracy, inclusion, citations, and competitive coverage over time.

How to use it

Leave with a decision.

The audit is useful only if it changes the order of work. Do not turn it into another unranked backlog.

01 · Observe

Record the current state

Use evidence, URLs, screenshots, and query outputs. Avoid scoring from memory.

02 · Rank

Separate cause from symptom

Prioritize issues that block access or create identity ambiguity before downstream publishing.

03 · Act

Choose the next constraint

Turn the highest-impact gaps into owners, acceptance criteria, and a review date.

What this is—and is not.

Does the audit guarantee AI visibility?

No. It helps you find and prioritize factors that influence access, understanding, corroboration, and measurement. Third-party AI outputs remain outside Swell's control.

Do I need to enter my email?

No. The self-audit opens directly in Notion. If you want help interpreting the results, email Mason or book a discovery call separately.

Is the paid diagnostic required before a monthly plan?

Not always. If your team already has a credible baseline, Swell can review it during scoping. Otherwise, the diagnostic prevents an ongoing program from starting with assumptions.

No. This is a Swell Marketing resource. AURE, at au-re.org, is a separate ecosystem organization and is not an alternate name or legal identity for Swell.
