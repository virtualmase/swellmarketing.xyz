Representation gap check

Start where the chain actually breaks.

Answer from evidence. A beautiful content program cannot compensate for an entity machines cannot identify. The chandelier is rarely the plumbing.

1. Can you point to one canonical identifier and a consistent description of the organization, its people, and its offers?
2. Can relevant crawlers retrieve the important facts and evidence from indexable, initial HTML?
3. Do your priority claims have specific first-party proof on pages that answer commercially relevant questions?
4. Do independent, relevant sources corroborate the claims and expertise you want associated with the brand?
5. Do you capture answers, citations, accuracy, and competitors against the same versioned query set over time?

Five dimensions

Audit the whole system.

A brand can have excellent content and still be hard to identify, crawl, corroborate, or measure. Work through the dimensions in order; upstream ambiguity can distort everything after it.

01

Entity clarity

Names, identifiers, structured data, people, offers, and relationships agree.

02

Crawler access

Important pages, facts, policies, and sitemaps are available to relevant systems.

03

Content evidence

Key claims are specific, supported, extractable, and connected to useful answers.

04

Independent authority

External sources corroborate the expertise and facts you want associated with the brand.

05

Answer measurement

A stable query set tracks accuracy, inclusion, citations, and competitive coverage over time.

How to use it

Audit questions

What this is and is not.

Does the audit guarantee AI visibility?

No. It helps you find and prioritize factors that influence access, understanding, corroboration, and measurement. Third-party AI outputs remain outside Swell's control.

Do I need to enter my email?

No. The self-audit opens directly in Notion. If you want help interpreting the results, request a GEO fit review separately.

Is a baseline review required before a monthly program?

Not always. If your team already has credible evidence, Swell can review it during fit review. Otherwise, a private baseline review may be proposed before Growth or Scale begins.
