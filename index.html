Frequently asked

The practical questions.

GEO is a young discipline, but the operating principle is straightforward: clarify the entity, publish useful evidence, earn corroboration, and measure the answers.

What is generative engine optimization?

Generative engine optimization, or GEO, is the practice of improving how a brand is represented in generative answers by clarifying entities, publishing accessible evidence, earning corroboration, and measuring observable answers.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO focuses on discovery and performance in search results. GEO adds measurement of answer inclusion, accuracy, attribution, citations, and named alternatives across relevant generative products. The disciplines overlap but use different observable outcomes.

How does a GEO engagement start?

Every engagement starts with a baseline audit across entity clarity, crawler access, content structure, authority signals, and current AI visibility. The resulting roadmap prioritizes the highest-impact fixes.

Can you guarantee that an AI platform will cite us?

No. Third-party systems control their outputs. Swell improves the conditions supporting accurate representation and citation, then compares observable answers against a stable query set under documented conditions.
