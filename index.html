Frequently asked

The practical questions.

GEO is a young discipline, but the operating principle is straightforward: clarify the entity, publish useful evidence, earn corroboration, and measure the answers.

What is generative engine optimization?

Generative engine optimization improves the technical, content, and authority signals that AI systems use to understand and cite a brand. It includes structured data, crawler access, entity consistency, citable content, independent corroboration, and query-level measurement.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO primarily improves visibility in ranked search results. GEO focuses on whether AI-generated answers understand your brand correctly, trust its claims, and cite it for relevant questions. Strong technical SEO still matters; GEO adds entity, evidence, and answer-surface considerations.

How does an engagement start?

We begin with a baseline across entity clarity, crawler access, content structure, authority signals, and current AI visibility. That baseline becomes a prioritized roadmap and the benchmark for later measurements.

Can you guarantee that an AI platform will cite us?

No responsible agency can guarantee a third-party model's output. We improve the conditions that support accurate representation and citation, then measure results against a consistent prompt set so progress is visible.
