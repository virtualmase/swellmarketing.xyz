01 // The Problem

Your Business Is Invisible to AI.
Here's Why.

You searched your company in ChatGPT. Nothing came back. A competitor showed up on Perplexity. Google's AI Overview didn't mention you once. This isn't a content problem. It's a signal architecture problem — and it has five root causes.

🔵
Undeclared Entity

No JSON-LD @graph with a stable @id URI means the LLM doesn't recognize you as an entity — it treats you as unverified text. You're a mention, not a node.

FIX TIME: 1 week · IMPACT: Highest
🔗
Zero sameAs Verification

LLMs weigh corroboration. If your entity isn't cross-referenced on LinkedIn, Crunchbase, Wikidata, and Google Business Profile, citation confidence stays near zero.

FIX TIME: 1 week · IMPACT: Very High
📄
Content Not LLM-Structured

Content written for human readers and content written for LLM citation are different things. If your key claims need surrounding context to make sense, they won't be extracted.

FIX TIME: 2–4 weeks · IMPACT: High
🚫
Crawlers Blocked or Uninvited

GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot. If your robots.txt doesn't explicitly allow them — and if you haven't deployed llms.txt — AI systems take a conservative approach and skip you.

FIX TIME: 1 day · IMPACT: High

02 // The Method

The GEO Architecture
That Compounds Over Time

Three layers, deployed in sequence. Each layer compounds the previous one. Most clients see measurable Share of Model movement within 30 days of infrastructure deployment.

01 // Foundation
Entity Architecture

Deploy a complete JSON-LD @graph with a stable @id URI, verified sameAs nodes across Tier 1–2 authority platforms, and a knowsAbout array mapping your topic authority claims. Test schema rendering via curl — never browser. LLM crawlers frequently don't execute JavaScript.

02 // Content
GEO Content Silos

Structure content around topical authority clusters optimized for AI extraction. Standalone citable paragraphs. FAQPage schema on every pillar. Entity-led opening sentences. Silo 3 first — urgency and discovery intent drives the fastest SoM movement.

03 // Distribution
Citation Amplification

Seed citations across LinkedIn, Medium, Quora, and Reddit with canonical back-references. Three independent source mentions close the E-E-A-T gap. Arctura Network distribution amplifies across the citation graph. External citations are the single highest-leverage signal for entity verification.
