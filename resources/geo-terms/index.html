00AI-native agency operations

AI-native digital agency AI-powered marketing agency
A digital agency whose research, production, orchestration, measurement, and knowledge systems are designed around governed AI workflows rather than added as isolated tools. Read the agency systems guide →
Autonomous digital agency Agentic marketing agency
An operating model in which verified workflows can advance work across acquisition, onboarding, campaigns, reporting, and follow-up within explicit scope, consent, budget, quality, and approval boundaries.
Agentic marketing workflow AI agent workflow
A versioned marketing process in which an agent can interpret state, use approved tools, produce evidence, and select permitted next actions under defined policy.
Campaign orchestration AI campaign management
The governed coordination of audience, assets, channels, approvals, tracking, budgets, stop conditions, measurement, and follow-up around one campaign objective.
Human approval gate Human in the loop
A required, recorded decision by an authorized person before an agent can make a material commitment or execute an action outside autonomous policy.
Marketing control plane Agency control plane
The lifecycle, policy, event, approval, and action layer that coordinates multiple systems without treating any single AI product as the source of truth.
AI concierge Voice AI concierge
A disclosed conversational interface that can answer approved questions, capture buyer context, route requests, and recommend verified next steps without impersonating a human or inventing commitments.
Closed-loop marketing Full-funnel measurement
A measurement practice connecting source, acquisition, sales, delivery, retention, and feedback events so later outcomes can improve future decisions.

01Discipline and category

Generative engine optimization GEO · AI search optimization
The practice of improving the clarity, accessibility, evidence, and corroboration that generative answer systems can use when representing an entity or topic. Read the GEO guide →
Answer engine optimization AEO · Answer optimization
A broader practice of structuring information so search and answer products can resolve a question directly. It overlaps with GEO, but the terms are not automatic synonyms in every context.
AI search visibility AI visibility · LLM visibility
The observed presence and treatment of an entity, claim, or source in defined AI-assisted search and answer experiences. Read the measurement guide →

02Representation and diagnosis

Representation operations Representation Ops
The ongoing discipline of aligning what an organization can prove with how answer systems describe, attribute, and cite it. See the Swell method →
Representation gap AI representation gap
A material difference between an observed answer and the current representation an organization can support with evidence. Run the free diagnostic →
Representation baseline AI answer baseline · GEO baseline
A versioned record of queries, conditions, answers, inclusion, accuracy, attribution, citations, and alternatives before or between interventions. Use the template →
First constraint Earliest constraint
The earliest verified failure across entity definition, access, evidence, corroboration, or measurement that should be resolved before downstream work.

03Entities and meaning

Entity optimization Brand entity optimization · Entity SEO
The work of making an organization, person, product, location, or concept unambiguous and consistently connected to supportable attributes and relationships. Read the entity guide →
Entity clarity Entity disambiguation
The degree to which a reader or system can distinguish an entity from similarly named, previous, parent, sibling, or related entities.
Entity architecture Entity graph architecture
The documented set of canonical entities, identifiers, attributes, and verified relationships used consistently across owned and relevant external sources.
Canonical entity Canonical brand entity
The authoritative identity record selected for one real organization, person, product, place, or concept, with a stable identifier and source.
Knowledge graph Entity graph
A structured network of entities and relationships. Public and private graphs differ, so markup should describe verified facts rather than presume adoption by a specific graph.
Structured data Schema markup · JSON-LD
Machine-readable markup that expresses page entities and properties using a shared vocabulary such as Schema.org. JSON-LD is one common syntax.
sameAs Identity links
A Schema.org property used to identify another URL representing the same entity, not a parent, partner, sibling, author, or merely related organization.

04Access and retrieval

Crawler access AI crawler access · Bot access
The effective ability of a documented crawler or user-directed fetcher to retrieve a public resource under the publisher's directives and infrastructure controls. Read the AI access-decision guide →
Retrieval Information retrieval
The process by which a system locates and selects information relevant to a request. Public optimization can improve accessible evidence but cannot guarantee retrieval.
llms.txt LLM text file
A proposed plain-text guide to important public site content. It can aid orientation but is not an access-control standard or a guaranteed ranking signal.

05Evidence and content

Citation readiness AI citation readiness
The degree to which a claim is explicit, attributable, accessible, current, scoped, and supported well enough to be inspected and potentially cited. Read the content guide →
Citable content Citation-ready content
Content that makes useful, supportable claims clear and inspectable without manufacturing authority or guaranteeing citation. Inspect the Evidence Contract →
Answer-ready content Extractable content
Content structured so a bounded question can be answered clearly in context. Answer readiness is a usability property, not proof of authority or future inclusion.
Evidence-led content Evidence-backed content
Content whose material claims are connected to appropriate sources, provenance, scope, ownership, and review dates.
Evidence ledger Claim-to-source map
A governed record connecting each material claim to source evidence, canonical publication, owner, status, and review date. Use the template →
Independent corroboration Third-party corroboration
Relevant support from a source outside the entity making the claim, evaluated for independence, quality, scope, and recency.

06Measurement and learning

AI citation LLM citation · Answer citation
A source reference presented by an AI-assisted answer product. Citation behavior differs by product, mode, query, date, and conditions.
Citation coverage AI citation share
The proportion of a versioned query portfolio for which a specified entity or source is cited under documented observation conditions.
Answer accuracy AI answer accuracy
The degree to which observed material claims match the current evidence available for the intended entity and scope.
Answer attribution Entity attribution
Whether an observed answer assigns a claim, product, credential, or relationship to the correct entity.
Query portfolio Prompt set · GEO query set
A versioned set of commercially relevant category, problem, comparison, and verification questions used for repeatable observation.
Share of Model SoM · AI answer share
A portfolio-level measure of how often an entity appears in defined answer observations. The denominator, products, prompts, dates, and counting rules must be disclosed.
Answer change log GEO change log
A versioned record connecting interventions to later observations while separating observed change from unproven causality. Use the template →
