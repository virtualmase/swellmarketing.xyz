Observe the answer. Disclose the conditions.

AI visibility is only useful when the entity, question set, product, mode, date, denominator, and counting rules travel with the number.

AI search visibility is the observed presence and treatment of an entity, claim, or source in defined AI-assisted search and answer experiences.

“How visible are we in AI?” is incomplete until the measurement specifies where, for which questions, for which entity and market, on what date, and under which mode or account conditions. Different products, and different experiences inside the same product, can produce different answers.

Build a query portfolio

A query portfolio is a versioned set of commercially relevant questions. It should reflect buyer research rather than a pile of brand prompts selected to produce favorable results.

IntentExample patternWhat it tests
CategoryWhich providers solve [problem] for [audience]?Inclusion and alternative presence
ProblemHow should a team address [specific constraint]?Topic association and source usefulness
ComparisonCompare [approach A] and [approach B] for [context].Claim accuracy, differentiation, citations
VerificationWhat does [entity] currently offer?Identity, recency, attribution
BrandedWho is [entity], and what is it known for?Canonical description and conflation

Preserve the requested query and the executed query if a system rewrites, clarifies, or disambiguates it. Version the portfolio when intent changes; do not silently edit history.

Create a representation baseline

For each observation, capture the answer product, surface or mode, model if disclosed, account/location conditions where material, exact query, timestamp, answer, citations, named entities, and errors. Store full evidence rather than reconstructing it from memory.

A baseline does not need to pretend that generative output is perfectly deterministic. It needs enough controls to make later comparisons honest about known variation.

Measure dimensions separately

MetricDefinitionExample counting rule
Inclusion rateShare of eligible observations in which the entity appearsEntity present ÷ eligible queries
Answer accuracyMaterial observed claims matching current evidenceSupported material claims ÷ assessed material claims
Answer attributionClaims assigned to the intended entityCorrectly attributed claims ÷ assessed claims
Citation coverageQueries citing a specified entity or source setQueries with qualifying citation ÷ eligible queries
Alternative presenceFrequency and context of other named entitiesAlternative appearances across the same portfolio

Always publish the numerator and denominator near a rate. Define “eligible,” “present,” “citation,” and “accurate” before collection.

Use Share of Model with disclosure

Share of Model is a portfolio-level measure of how often an entity appears in defined answer observations. It is not a universal market-share number.

A defensible report states the entities counted, query portfolio and version, products/surfaces, observation window, number of runs, eligibility rules, treatment of duplicate mentions, and whether position or prominence is included. Changing any of those can change the number without a real change in market recognition.

Treat citations as observations

An AI citation is a source reference presented by an answer product. A citation can support one sentence, a broader passage, or simply provide adjacent reading; inspect it before scoring support.

Useful citation analysis records:

  • The cited URL, publisher, page type, and publication state.
  • Which answer claim the source appears to support.
  • Whether the source actually supports that claim.
  • Owned versus independent source status.
  • Recency, canonicalization, and entity accuracy.
  • Repeated source patterns across the stable query portfolio.

Separate change from causality

An intervention can precede an answer change without causing it. Products update models, indexes, ranking systems, interfaces, and retrieval behavior outside the publisher’s control. Sampling can also vary.

Use an Answer Change Log to record what shipped, when it became retrievable, what changed later, what else could explain the difference, and the confidence appropriate to the evidence. Prefer “the observed citation changed after publication” over “this page forced the model to cite us.”

A useful operating scorecard

  • Query portfolio coverage and observation completion.
  • Inclusion, accuracy, attribution, and citation coverage by intent.
  • Material representation gaps opened and resolved.
  • Evidence and entity defects found during investigation.
  • Interventions shipped with acceptance evidence.
  • Qualified commercial actions connected to the content or answer journey.
  • Known limitations, failures, and products not observed.
