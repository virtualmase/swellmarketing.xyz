Build a list worth checking.

Answer from public evidence where possible. “Not yet” and “Unsure” become prompts for inspection; they do not diagnose an organization or make a claim about any search, AI, or browser-agent system.

1. Is there one public page that names the organization, its current offer, and the canonical URL you want people to use?
2. Do relevant official profiles or public indexes point to the same organization without inventing an affiliation?
3. Can a reader inspect the source, date, method, and limitation behind a material public claim?
4. Does structured data describe the same visible page content rather than hidden or unrelated statements?
5. Can a visitor reach the important facts through ordinary, descriptive links on public pages?
6. Are the next actions on your site real links or buttons with clear, visible labels and stable placement?
7. Can a qualified buyer distinguish current scope, dependencies, and the next consultative action without guessing?
8. Is there an accountable owner and review date for changes to important public statements?

    Sources and transparent references

    Reference role: an external link explains why a source, profile, listing, or example is relevant. It does not establish endorsement, affiliation, authority, ranking, or a commercial result.

    Primary sources

    The map’s source and visible-content rules follow Google Search Central’s guide to generative AI features and structured-data guidelines. Its interface questions draw on web.dev’s agent-friendly website guidance. Crawler-policy distinctions use OpenAI’s crawler documentation. These are primary-source citations, not endorsements of Swell.

    Identity references

    Schema.org describes Organization and its `sameAs` property as a way to identify an unambiguous reference page. Use an official website, verified profile, or public registry only when it genuinely identifies the organization named on the page. A profile link is an identity reference; it is not proof of quality or inclusion in a third-party system.

    Independent examples

    Swell may cite an independent company, publisher, or competitor when a specific public implementation helps explain a bounded practice. Such links are labeled “Independent example - not affiliated.” We do not use competitor links to imply superiority, infer a relationship, or manufacture a comparative claim.

    What the map does not determine

    This worksheet does not crawl a site, validate legal identity, test individual bots, evaluate a competitor, qualify a lead, or predict search ranking, citation, traffic, conversion, or any AI output. It is a private prompt for the next piece of evidence worth gathering.
