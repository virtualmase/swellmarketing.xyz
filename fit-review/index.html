Fit-review preparation

Bring the decision. Not a dossier.

What a fit review is

A short consultative conversation to decide whether self-guided work, a private baseline review, Swell GEO Growth, or Swell GEO Scale is appropriate. It is not a free audit, and it does not require confidential documents.

Three useful inputs

Show us what is costly to leave unclear.

Preparation is lightweight. Public pages and a clearly owned decision are enough to make the conversation useful.

01 / Decision

One current business question

Bring the buyer answer, offer, launch, entity relationship, public claim, or discovery problem that needs a decision. Describe why it matters now and who can act on the result.

02 / Source

One public page or observed answer

Bring the public URL, product page, knowledge source, or observed answer that shows the constraint. Do not send private credentials, client files, or sensitive records.

03 / Constraints

Your review and approval reality

Share the relevant decision owner, approval boundary, delivery capacity, and timing constraint. The right scope depends on what your team can review and operate.

Useful boundary: A fit review establishes whether there is enough context to recommend a next step. It does not produce a public score, guarantee a platform outcome, or replace a scoped baseline where one is needed.
The ordered path

Check. Fit review. Written scope. Private collection.

The process stays consultative because the work must have an accountable decision, evidence boundary, and delivery owner.

01 / Check

Use the open diagnostic

Start self-guided if your team can gather the public evidence and rank the first constraint.

02 / Fit review

Confirm decision context

Bring the three inputs above. We determine whether a scoped operating program fits.

03 / Written scope

Agree the work

A recommendation becomes an engagement only through an accepted, versioned scope.

04 / Private collection

Collect after acceptance

Private collection instructions follow written acceptance. There is no public self-service checkout.
