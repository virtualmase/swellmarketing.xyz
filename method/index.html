Five phases

A closed learning loop.

The sequence matters. Producing content before resolving identity and access issues creates more pages without solving the reason they are overlooked.

01 · Baseline

Define the answer surface

Agree on buyers, commercial questions, platforms, competitors, and what a correct answer should include. Capture the initial results before changing anything.

02 · Clarify

Resolve identity and access

Repair canonical identifiers, structured data, crawler policy, source consistency, and initial-HTML availability.

03 · Build

Publish useful evidence

Create pages that answer specific questions with clear claims, definitions, methods, sources, and internal relationships.

04 · Prove

Strengthen corroboration

Close authority gaps through real profiles, expert attribution, earned mentions, and distribution where the audience already learns.

05 · Learn

Re-run and prioritize

Measure the same prompt portfolio, review accuracy and citation changes, document what moved, and select the next constraint.

Quality gates

What must be true before launch.

Pages ship only when humans and machines can both understand what the page claims, why it is credible, and where to go next.

Clarity

One page, one job

A defined audience question, one primary action, and a heading hierarchy that exposes the answer.

Evidence

Claims earn support

Specific claims are sourced, qualified, or labeled as methodology not dressed up as fabricated proof.

Continuity

No dead ends

Every page connects to the next useful action and every conversion route is tested end to end.

What we do not promise

No agency controls a third-party model's answer. Swell does not guarantee citations, manufacture “live” proof, or substitute activity volume for measurable progress. We improve the conditions for accurate representation and report what actually changes.
