The handoff record

Inspectable work. Clear ownership.

A partner handoff is useful only when the buyer, sources, questions, dependencies, and next decision are explicit.

01 · Define

Entity transition map

Current and former names, domains, products, legal relationships, effective dates, and approved canonical sources.

02 · Observe

Versioned question record

Selected buyer questions, answer conditions, citations, material mismatches, and explicit unknowns.

03 · Decide

Constraint and owner

The earliest entity, access, evidence, corroboration, or measurement constraint with acceptance evidence and an accountable next action.

One reversible test

Start with a pattern. Not a partnership contract.

The first conversation uses public or anonymized evidence. No client list, credentials, private files, or exclusive commitment is required.

Step 01

Bring one transition

Name the company/product change, current source of truth, affected buyer question, and why verification matters now.

Step 02

Confirm permission and boundaries

The client approves any introduction or source sharing. Each party's scope, prohibited claims, and data boundary remain explicit.

Step 03

Choose the next record

Use the self-guided diagnostic, define a privately scoped Baseline, prepare managed work, or close with no further action.

Step 04

Hand the evidence back

The originating partner receives the approved findings and ownership record needed for its ongoing work.

Commercial boundary

No hidden channel terms.

There is no referral fee, exclusivity, white-label promise, reciprocal list sharing, or client-data transfer unless separately approved in writing. Public relevance is never treated as contact permission.

What Swell can contract for

Defined evidence artifacts, observation conditions, controllable technical and content work, governance, and a review cadence. Swell cannot guarantee rankings, citations, model inclusion, traffic, leads, revenue, or a specific third-party answer.
