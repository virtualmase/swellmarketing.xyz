Questions

Before you choose.

What is the difference between the free diagnostic and a baseline review?

The free diagnostic is a self-guided orientation. A baseline review is proposed privately only when the available evidence is insufficient to scope the appropriate operating program.

Are the monthly programs available through public checkout?

No. Growth and Scale are consultative services. Scope, delivery capacity, dependencies, acceptance criteria, and commercial terms are confirmed in writing before any private collection instruction is sent.

Can Swell guarantee an AI citation?

No. AI platforms control their outputs. Swell improves the clarity, evidence, accessibility, and authority signals that support accurate representation, then measures documented observations.

Is the Academy included?

Academy training is a separate educational product with its own enrollment path. Agency programs are scoped services delivered for your brand.
