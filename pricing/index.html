Questions

Before you choose.

What is the difference between the free audit and paid diagnostic?

The free audit is a self-guided template. The paid diagnostic is completed by Swell and includes brand-specific research, a documented baseline, prioritized findings, and an implementation readout.

Are these fixed packages?

The listed rates are starting points. Final scope depends on the number of entities, sites, markets, content surfaces, and queries being measured. Those variables are confirmed in writing.

Can Swell guarantee an AI citation?

No. AI platforms control their outputs. Swell improves the clarity, evidence, accessibility, and authority signals that support accurate representation, then measures changes consistently.

Is the Academy included?

Academy training is a separate educational product with its own enrollment path. Agency plans are scoped services delivered for your brand.

Bring the costly answer.

We will tell you whether it needs a self-guided audit, a commissioned baseline, or an operating program. Sometimes the correct scope is smaller.
