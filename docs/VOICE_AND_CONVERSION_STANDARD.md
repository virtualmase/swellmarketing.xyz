# Swell Voice and Conversion Standard

## Voice

The voice reports. It does not perform intelligence.

- Open with one concrete analogy or commercial observation.
- Follow with the precise mechanism, definition, or evidence.
- Use periods. Avoid em dashes.
- Prefer declarative sentences over connective explanation.
- Use dry humor as an observation, once. Never explain the joke.
- Create emotion through consequence and contrast. Do not tell the reader what to feel.
- Preserve machine-readable structure, definitions, evidence, and schema beneath the human layer.
- Make every proprietary term useful enough to repeat without Swell in the room.

The analogy is the invitation. The evidence is the credential.

## Conversion

Every high-intent page must answer five questions in order:

1. What costly or uncomfortable problem does the buyer recognize?
2. What mechanism explains it without jargon?
3. What does Swell do that changes the condition?
4. What proof, boundary, or price makes the offer credible?
5. What is the smallest useful next action?

Use one primary CTA per section. Prefer a concrete verb and result:

- Run the free diagnostic
- Book a working session
- Send the representation gap
- Open the evidence template

Avoid vague CTAs such as learn more, get started, explore solutions, or unlock growth.

## Anti-slop test

Delete or rewrite copy that could appear unchanged on a generic agency website. Each section must contain at least one of:

- A proprietary operating concept
- A specific observable outcome
- A commercial consequence
- An inspectable artifact
- A clear boundary or honest limitation

Signal gives the reader coordinates. Slop gives the reader adjectives.
