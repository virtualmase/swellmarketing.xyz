# Sales Sequence Library

These are human-reviewed starting points. Personalize from verified context. Do not use them for bulk unsolicited messaging or claim an action that did not occur.

## Inbound request — immediate acknowledgement

**Subject:** Swell received your representation request

Hi {{first_name}},

I received your note about {{buyer_trigger_short}}. I’m reviewing the affected company/entity, the commercial consequence you described, and the earliest layer worth verifying.

I’ll respond with one recommended next step by {{response_deadline}}. If anything in the request is sensitive or should not be retained, reply here and say so.

— Mason

Automation may send this only after the lead destination confirms the request was saved.

## No-show

Maximum automated no-show attempts: one. Additional outreach requires human judgment and valid consent.

If this was recorded incorrectly, contact Mason directly at masonnguyengeo@gmail.com.

## Quality gate

- [ ] The recipient, context, consent basis, and source are known.
- [ ] Variables are populated from verified records.
- [ ] The message contains one decision or next action.
- [ ] Any link/action has been confirmed by its system.
- [ ] Attempt limits and opt-outs are enforced across channels.
- [ ] The message does not manufacture urgency, evidence, familiarity, or results.
