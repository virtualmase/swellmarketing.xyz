## Evidence and reasoning

### SOLO MEDIA GROUP — advance to relationship-basis check

- SOLO presents itself as a Shopify Platinum agency and describes full migrations, brand discovery/design, integrations, analytics, and B2B wholesale work. Those capabilities create a plausible handoff after a commerce replatform, consolidation, or identity change.
- Its public partner ecosystem supports the possibility of complementary delivery, but it does not grant Swell contact permission or prove a client-introduction process.
- The fit is deliberately restricted to commerce-platform transitions. It does not establish fit for the broader B2B software cohort.
- Sources: [official site](https://solomediagroup.com/), [Shopify partner profile](https://www.shopify.com/partners/directory/partner/solo-media-group1), [Stripe Directory profile](https://stri.pe/sl/ltJo0yOv).

**Next permitted action:** Check the founder's existing network, CRM history, and mutual connections for a real relationship basis. If none exists, wait for an opt-in event, reply, or introduction; do not use directory data as permission.

### Argon Agency — hold

- Argon publicly offers strategy, research, product launch, content and messaging, brand positioning, branding, website UX/UI, and PR. This covers much of the transition execution criterion.
- Its visible specialization leans toward healthcare, behavioral health, and addiction treatment rather than B2B software. That makes it a possible later vertical partner, not a first-cohort channel.
- No reviewed evidence established a client-consent path, source-governance workflow, or accountable partner owner for this program.
- Sources: [official site](https://argonagency.com/), [Stripe Directory profile](https://stri.pe/sl/aayZCUaS).

**Re-entry condition:** A verified healthcare technology transition case plus a warm relationship basis.

### Rebrandery — hold

- Public descriptions support web design plus SEO/AEO adjacency, but the research did not establish a portfolio of B2B software name, domain, product, or category transitions.
- A brand-aligned company name is not evidence of rebrand execution. The score therefore reflects only the capabilities that could be verified.
- Sources: [official site](https://www.rebrandery.com/), [Stripe Directory profile](https://stri.pe/sl/Gf9izeVs).

**Re-entry condition:** Two relevant transition examples, a named delivery owner, and a legitimate relationship basis.

### BAK MEDIA INC and Dynamik Endeavors — hold

- Stripe Directory descriptions indicate broad web/AI/brand or visual-branding capabilities, but not enough specialization or independently verifiable evidence for the active campaign.
- Sources: [BAK profile](https://stri.pe/sl/w4ZNOOlG), [Dynamik profile](https://stri.pe/sl/XDH90wRE).

**Re-entry condition:** Public transition cases and technical B2B buyer evidence. Do not spend manual research time before the higher-scoring candidates produce a signal.

## Operating queue

1. Search only owner-controlled CRM notes and the founder's known network for a SOLO relationship basis.
2. If a legitimate basis exists, prepare a human-reviewed, one-to-one request to compare an anonymized commerce-transition handoff. Record the basis before sending.
3. If no basis exists, move SOLO to passive nurture and direct qualified traffic to `/partners/`; do not scrape or enrich contacts.
4. Keep Argon reserved for a healthcare-technology transition signal.
5. Do not research the remaining candidates further until the channel records either one consented introduction or five permitted relationship-development actions.

## CRM minimum record

Before a conversation, record: candidate, fit score, evidence URLs, narrow use case, relationship basis, consent status, partner owner, last human review, next permitted action, and disqualifier. Before an introduction, separately record buyer permission, partner permission, transition trigger, source-sharing boundaries, and original-touch attribution.
