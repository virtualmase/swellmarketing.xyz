# Name-Change Representation Research Queue

## Purpose and boundary

This is the market-intelligence and account-research brief for the first focused outbound experiment. It is private operating material. Inclusion means only that a public company announcement documents a relevant change. It does not mean that an AI answer is inaccurate, that Swell has performed an audit, or that the company has consented to contact.

Do not publish account-level observations. Do not enrich personal email addresses, scrape contact data, or place any account into a sequence from this file. Public relevance is not outreach permission.

## ICP audit

The current ICP is directionally sound but too broad for a first campaign. “B2B software or technical service with a change in the last 24 months” combines several different problems:

- A company rename creates entity-resolution and domain-transition questions.
- A product rename creates product-to-company relationship questions.
- A category expansion creates claim and corroboration questions.
- A visual rebrand without a name or offer change may create no material representation problem.

These triggers should not share one initial message. The first campaign should isolate the clearest event: a company name change paired with a domain or category change.

## First campaign segment

**Segment:** B2B software companies that publicly changed company names within the last eight months and also changed a canonical domain, category description, or company-to-product relationship.

**Buyer:** The person publicly responsible for corporate marketing, brand, communications, or product marketing. A founder is appropriate only when the company has no evident functional owner.

**Buyer job:** Verify that a recent identity change is consistently represented where prospective buyers research the company, then create a versioned evidence record for any material mismatch.

**Why this segment:** A name transition provides an inspectable old state and new state. The consequence can be discussed without alleging a problem: buyer confusion, incorrect vendor comparison, or conflation of the old and new entities. It also maps directly to Swell's entity-definition layer.

**Offer path:** Permission-based Representation Transition Review -> working session -> commissioned Representation Baseline when the buyer confirms a material issue and lacks a credible baseline. The public commercial menu remains GEO Growth at $2,500/month and GEO Scale at $3,500/month. This brief does not authorize a new public offer or price.

## Account-selection criteria

Score only from preserved public evidence. Unknown values stay unknown; they do not receive optimistic points.

| Criterion | Points | Evidence required |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Company name changed in the last eight months | 25 | Dated issuer announcement naming old and new company |
| Canonical domain changed | 15 | Old and new domains in issuer-controlled evidence or redirect observation |
| Category or product scope changed materially | 15 | Issuer describes the former and current positioning |
| B2B software is central to the offer | 10 | Current canonical product/company page |
| Research confusion has a plausible commercial consequence | 10 | Written hypothesis tied to a buyer decision, not a generic visibility claim |
| Multiple entities/products require explicit relationships | 10 | Current portfolio or company/product structure |
| Named functional buyer can be identified from a company-controlled source | 5 | Current leadership, newsroom, or author page |
| North American or English-language selling motion | 5 | Current company-controlled market evidence |
| A permitted contact path exists | 5 | Inbound request, introduction, event interaction, subscribed relationship, or other documented legitimate basis |

Research priority begins at 70/100. Contact is still prohibited when the permission criterion is zero. Disqualify visual-only rebrands, consumer businesses, companies with no plausible buyer-research consequence, guarantee-seeking requests, suppressed accounts, and any situation requiring personal-data scraping.

## Prospect research workflow

1. **Register the trigger.** Save the issuer URL, publication date, old name, new name, old domain, new domain, stated category change, and exact passages needed to reproduce the record. A syndicated release is evidence of what the issuer announced, not independent corroboration.
2. **Verify the current canonical state.** Inspect the current homepage, about page, product pages, structured data, title/description, and official profiles. Record redirects without treating them as proof of indexing or answer behavior.
3. **Map entities.** Write the company, product, parent, acquired brands, and old/new relationships as explicit statements. Stop when the intended relationship cannot be established from company-controlled evidence.
4. **Form three versioned research questions.** Use one exact-name question, one old-name transition question, and one category question. Preserve query, surface, date, locale, account state, answer, citations, and screenshots or exports where permitted.
5. **Classify, do not dramatize.** Mark each observation aligned, mixed, partial, legacy-heavy, or unresolved. Public search results are a control and must not be called AI-answer evidence.
6. **Test commercial relevance.** State the buyer decision that could be affected and the evidence supporting that inference. “May reduce visibility” is insufficient.
7. **Identify the role, not a personal inbox.** Use company leadership/newsroom pages or a professional network to identify a likely function. Do not append guessed emails or sensitive personal data.
8. **Check permission and suppression.** Record the specific contact basis, source, scope, date, channel, attempt cap, and opt-out state. No basis means research-only.
9. **Human review.** A human approves the evidence summary and message. The message must say that no inaccuracy has been established unless a reproducible baseline supports that statement.
10. **One contextual attempt.** Use the approved channel and one decision-oriented message. Do not follow up without a reply or pre-existing permission. Log the outcome and next action.

## Holdout accounts

- Optimizely and PlanSource announced brand evolutions without a company-name transition. Keep them outside this experiment so a visual/positioning refresh does not dilute the name-change hypothesis.
- Syspro announced a new global brand and platform modernization but apparently retained its company name. It may fit a later category-repositioning cohort.
- Older changes such as 4me -> Xurrent fall outside the eight-month trigger window even if they remain useful method examples.

## First five account packets

Build packets in this order: Airspeed, Crosstie, BlueDolphin, CWILL, and Illumia. Each packet must contain:

- One preserved issuer announcement.
- Current canonical company and product evidence.
- An entity relationship map.
- Three versioned questions and observation conditions.
- One commercial-consequence hypothesis labeled as inference.
- A likely buyer function.
- Permission/suppression status.
- A research decision: advance, hold, or disqualify.

Do not draft personalized outreach until the packet has a nonzero permission basis. If no permitted path exists across the first five, the campaign decision is “change distribution,” not “send anyway.” Use organic publication, opted-in diagnostic requests, partners, or consented event interactions to create the path.

## Campaign measurement

**Primary:** connected buyer conversations, qualified opportunities, paid baselines sold, and qualified pipeline value.

**Leading:** completed account packets, accounts with a reproducible material observation, accounts with a permitted contact path, and contextual attempts.

**Guardrails:** unsupported allegations, contact without a documented basis, opt-out/suppression failure, low-fit rate, and records without a dated next action.

Review after five complete packets and either five permitted contextual attempts or a documented finding that no permitted distribution path exists. Do not treat the absence of permission as evidence that the segment or message failed.
