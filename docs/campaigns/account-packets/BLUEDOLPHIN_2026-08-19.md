## Current canonical record

Company-controlled pages currently describe BlueDolphin as:

- An AI-native or AI-powered business-transformation platform
- One environment connecting strategic planning, solution design, and architecture management
- The current unified brand for the company formerly known as ValueBlue
- A platform serving global organizations from offices including New York and Utrecht

The announcement says the product had long been named BlueDolphin before the company adopted the same name. This means historical references to “BlueDolphin by ValueBlue” may be accurate for the earlier period, while current descriptions should distinguish historical from current identity.
