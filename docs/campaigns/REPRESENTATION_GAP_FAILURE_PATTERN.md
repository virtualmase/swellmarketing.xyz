## Hypothesis

Evidence-rich B2B companies with outdated AI descriptions will engage more often with a concrete failure-pattern observation and Representation Gap diagnostic than with generic GEO-services messaging.

This is a prospective experiment, not an established claim. The target-account set documents public changes that make representation worth checking. It does not claim that any named company is currently represented inaccurately.

## Target segment

- B2B software or technical-service company.
- Documented product, category, brand, or market change within the last 24 months.
- Public first-party evidence describing the change.
- Buyer research plausibly occurs through search or AI products.
- A marketing, communications, product marketing, growth, or founder owner could validate the current representation if a problem is observed.

Disqualify any account when no commercial consequence is plausible, evidence access is unavailable, the request depends on a guarantee, or contact would violate consent or suppression rules.

## Buyer problem

A company can change faster than the descriptions used to evaluate it. A renamed company may still be described under the old entity. A newly expanded platform may still be summarized as its former point product. A launched capability may appear in one canonical announcement while older descriptions continue to dominate elsewhere.

The commercial question is not whether an answer sounds imperfect. It is whether the answer creates a material misunderstanding during buyer research.

## Public-search control observation

On 2026-08-10, SWELL executed the prepared 30 questions as a public-search control across the ten-account research set. This was not an observation of OpenAI, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, or any other AI answer surface.

- 29 of 30 questions surfaced at least one current first-party source.
- 25 observations aligned with the documented current state.
- Two were mixed, two were partial, and one was legacy-heavy.
- Three generic questions required entity disambiguation; the requested and executed query were preserved separately.

The control rejects an overbroad discoverability-failure premise. The narrower pattern worth testing is whether broad category language and ambiguous entities make recent changes less consistently represented in actual answer surfaces. The versioned observation record is `data/representation-gap-public-search-control.json`.

## Public article copy

### When AI still describes the product you retired

Your company launches the new product on Monday. The old description keeps taking meetings for another year.

That does not prove an AI system is broken. It proves that a company change and an observed answer are different records with different clocks.

The first question is not, “How do we publish more?” The first question is, “Where does the representation stop matching what we can prove?”

The gap usually starts in one of five places:

1. **Entity definition.** The old and new company, product, or category relationships are unclear.
2. **Access.** The current evidence exists, but a retrieval system cannot reliably reach or interpret it.
3. **Evidence.** Important claims are scattered, implicit, or unsupported on the canonical pages.
4. **Corroboration.** Independent sources still describe the previous state or do not support the new one.
5. **Measurement.** Nobody has preserved the query, answer, date, surface, citations, and comparison conditions.

Publishing downstream content cannot reliably repair an upstream identity or evidence problem. More pages can create a larger haystack while the same needle remains missing.

SWELL starts with a versioned Representation Baseline. It records what selected AI products say under documented conditions, connects material claims to inspectable evidence, locates the earliest constraint, and measures again after corrective work.

That process improves controllable evidence and operations. It does not guarantee rankings, citations, inclusion, traffic, pipeline, or revenue.

If an answer still describes the company you used to be, run the Representation Gap diagnostic and identify the first layer worth verifying.

## Minimum review evidence

Do not interpret the hypothesis before all three are true:

- Ten target accounts have a completed first-party change record.
- Ten permitted distribution attempts or an equivalent documented organic reach test have occurred.
- At least three connected buyer conversations have been recorded, unless the distribution gate itself is the demonstrated constraint.

## Decision rule

- **Continue:** Specific problem-led conversations reach the target segment and qualification evidence improves.
- **Change message or segment:** Conversations occur, but the commercial consequence or evidence-access dimensions repeatedly fail.
- **Change distribution:** Relevant buyers do not reach the asset, while the asset and landing path pass their quality gates.
- **Stop:** Governance failures occur, the premise requires unsupported claims, or the minimum evidence window closes without a viable permitted distribution path.
