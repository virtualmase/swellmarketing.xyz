## Partner thesis

Brand, communications, product-marketing, web-migration, and implementation partners already encounter the trigger Swell is studying. Swell can complement their work by preserving buyer-facing answer conditions, mapping company/product relationships, locating the earliest representation constraint, and documenting post-change observations.

The partner is not asked to sell “guaranteed AI visibility.” The useful handoff is a client with:

- A dated name, domain, product, merger, or category transition
- A specific buyer-facing answer or research concern
- A current canonical source and an owner who can validate it
- A commercial or operating consequence inside 90 days
- Permission for an introduction

## Mutual value

### Swell contributes

- An ungated five-layer Representation Gap diagnostic
- A privately scoped Representation Baseline when no credible observation record exists
- Entity and evidence mapping that protects transition intent
- Clear non-promises and documented acceptance criteria
- A handback record the originating partner can use in ongoing brand, PR, product-marketing, or web work

### The partner contributes

- Verified transition context
- A consented introduction to the accountable buyer
- Clarity about its own scope and client obligations
- Source evidence and approval boundaries where the client permits

No referral fee, exclusivity, white-label promise, client claim, or data-sharing arrangement exists unless separately approved in writing.

## Partner call agenda

1. Which company/product transitions does the partner actually own?
2. When does its current scope end?
3. Which buyer-facing answer or evidence problems appear after launch?
4. What client permission is needed for an introduction or source sharing?
5. What will Swell deliver and hand back?
6. Which claims, data, and client relationships remain prohibited?
7. What is the first reversible test?

The first test is one anonymized pattern review or one consented introduction. Do not begin with exclusivity, automation, reciprocal list sharing, or a referral-fee scheme.
