# Demand Generation Playbook

## Objective

Create qualified conversations with companies experiencing a commercially meaningful representation problem. Optimize for qualified pipeline and paid baselines, not traffic, impressions, downloads, or undifferentiated lead volume.

## Message hierarchy

1. **Problem:** AI products may describe a company inaccurately, incompletely, or inconsistently with what it can prove.
2. **Consequence:** Buyers use those answers to form shortlists, objections, and expectations.
3. **Diagnosis:** Representation problems occur across entity definition, access, evidence, corroboration, and measurement; downstream publishing cannot reliably compensate for an upstream constraint.
4. **Method:** Establish a versioned baseline, resolve the earliest constraint, verify the change, and continue the operating loop.
5. **Boundary:** Swell improves controllable evidence and operations; it does not guarantee third-party answers.
6. **Action:** Use the ungated diagnostic, request a working session, or commission the baseline.

## Channel roles

| Channel | Role | Primary asset/action | Qualification signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Organic search | Capture named problems and methodology questions | Evidence-led answer pages and diagnostic | High-intent page path plus request |
| Organic AI | Make claims and entity relationships verifiable | Canonical pages, schema, evidence artifacts | Self-reported AI referral plus landing path |
| Mason research | Establish original concepts and definitions | Canonical research linked to Swell application | Referral from a relevant research asset |
| LinkedIn | Surface buyer observations and operating lessons | Evidence-backed posts and teardown invitations | Specific problem shared in reply/DM/form |
| Referral/partner | Transfer trust and problem context | Partner brief and direct introduction | Named introducer plus commercial trigger |
| Email nurture | Help a consented lead make the next decision | Short evidence assets tied to stated constraint | Reply, diagnostic completion, or meeting |
| Warm outbound | Follow up on a requested or legitimate business interaction | Human-led contextual message; AI only after consent review | Two-way response and explicit interest |

Do not launch broad autonomous voice outreach. Any outbound program requires a documented consent/legal basis, suppression controls, identity disclosure, time-zone rules, attempt limits, and human ownership.

## Content portfolio

Maintain four asset types only:

### Problem evidence

Shows a real failure pattern without fabricating prevalence. Examples: outdated product descriptions, entity conflation, unsupported recommendation claims, or volatile answer measurement.

### Decision tools

Helps a buyer decide what to verify or do next. The Representation Gap diagnostic and operating templates belong here.

### Method evidence

Explains acceptance criteria, provenance, baseline design, measurement, and why work is sequenced.

### Commercial evidence

Clarifies fit, scope, responsibilities, exclusions, pricing logic, and what happens during a paid engagement.

Retire or revise an asset that cannot be tied to a buyer question, objection, pipeline stage, or evidence gap.

## Initial 90-day campaign

### Hypothesis

Growth-stage B2B companies that encounter outdated or inaccurate AI descriptions will engage with a concrete teardown and baseline offer more readily than with generic “GEO services” messaging.

### Segment

- B2B software or technical service
- Meaningful product/category change in the last 24 months
- Evidence-rich site but possible inconsistency across external sources
- Buyer research plausibly occurs through search/AI
- Named marketing, growth, product marketing, communications, or founder owner

### Offer path

1. Public post or research asset names a failure pattern.
2. Ungated diagnostic helps the buyer locate the earliest layer.
3. Request form captures the observed problem, consequence, timing, attribution, and consent.
4. Qualified buyers receive a working session.
5. Buyers without a credible baseline receive the commissioned Representation Baseline offer.

### Weekly publishing rhythm

Cadence is capacity-dependent, not a ranking claim:

- One buyer-observation post
- One evidence/method artifact or meaningful update
- One direct distribution pass to relevant, consented, or contextual audiences
- One pipeline-learning review that decides the next topic

### Primary metrics

- Qualified opportunities created
- Paid baselines sold
- Qualified pipeline value
- Source-to-qualified rate
- Source-to-revenue

### Guardrails

- Unsubscribe/opt-out rate
- Unsupported claim count
- Low-fit lead rate
- Content without a documented pipeline purpose
- Follow-up SLA failures

### Stop/change rule

After a minimum review window agreed before launch, change the message or segment when conversations occur but qualification fails repeatedly. Change distribution when the right buyer does not reach the asset. Do not infer channel failure from raw traffic alone.

## Campaign naming and attribution

Use lowercase snake-case values:

```text
utm_source=linkedin
utm_medium=organic_social
utm_campaign=representation_gap_q3_2026
utm_content=outdated_product_description
```

Never overwrite original-touch attribution. Store original and latest touch separately in the CRM; create an activity for meaningful intermediate touches.

## Partner/referral brief

Give partners a one-page explanation containing:

- Who Swell is for
- Observable triggers
- Disqualifiers and non-promises
- The ungated diagnostic
- The commissioned baseline and starting price
- How to introduce a buyer with consent
- How referral attribution will be preserved

Do not create undisclosed incentives or ask a partner to make claims Swell cannot support.

## Quality gate

- [ ] Every campaign has a segment, problem, offer, owner, dates, attribution, consent basis, metrics, and stop rule.
- [ ] The landing experience matches the campaign claim.
- [ ] The CRM can trace source through revenue.
- [ ] Follow-up capacity exists before launch.
- [ ] Distribution is contextual and permitted.
- [ ] Learning is based on qualified pipeline evidence, not vanity metrics.
