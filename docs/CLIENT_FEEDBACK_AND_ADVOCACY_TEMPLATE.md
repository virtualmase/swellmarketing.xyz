## Private feedback request

Ask neutrally. Do not offer an incentive conditional on positive sentiment and do not route only happy clients to an independent review platform in a way that violates platform policy.

- What was most useful?
- What fell short or created friction?
- What changed for the team or business?
- What should we do differently next cycle?
- Is there anything still unresolved?
