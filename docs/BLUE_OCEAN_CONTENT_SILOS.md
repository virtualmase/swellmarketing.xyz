## Editorial rules

1. Lead with the buyer's observed problem, not an acronym.
2. Define the condition, evidence required, workflow owner, decision, and boundary.
3. Link each new page upward to one silo hub, sideways to one method or evidence asset, and forward to one appropriate diagnostic or service.
4. Use ecosystem projects only where their actual state illustrates the workflow. A preview is a preview; testnet is testnet; related entities remain distinct.
5. Treat “GEO,” “AEO,” “LLMO,” and “AI SEO” as discovery aliases where useful. Swell's differentiated territory is representation operations.
6. Never guarantee rankings, citations, recommendations, traffic, pipeline, or revenue from a content or technical change.
